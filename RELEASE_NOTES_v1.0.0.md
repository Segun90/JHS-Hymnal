## 🌟 Welcome to J.H.S Bible & Hymnal (Version 1.0.0)

We are delighted to introduce the official initial release of **J.H.S Bible & Hymnal** — an all-in-one, 100% offline spiritual sanctuary designed for Christian worship, personal devotion, church services, and scripture study.

---

## 📖 Key Features & Highlights

### 🎵 1. Complete Authoritative Hymnal (375 Hymns)
- **375 Verified Hymns:** Extracted from the authoritative print source with stanzas, choruses, refrains, and original phrasing intact.
- **22 Topical Categories:** Comprehensive spiritual categorizations including *Admonition, Adoration, Assurance & Confidence, Blood of Jesus, Christian Life, Christian Service & Reward, Christ Our Saviour, Commitment & Consecration, Conflict & Victory, Divine Healing, Evangelism, Grace & Forgiveness, Heaven, Holy Spirit, Invitation, Prayer, Rapture, Sanctification, Special Occasions, The Lord's Love & Care, Warning & Judgment,* and *Supplementary Hymns & Songs*.
- **Quick Hymn Dial Pad:** Direct numeric keypad to jump to any hymn in seconds.
- **Three Comprehensive Indexes:**
  - **Topical Index:** Grouped neatly by spiritual theme.
  - **General Index:** Alphabetical list of all hymns by title.
  - **First Lines Index:** Alphabetical list by the first line of each hymn.
- **Easy Hymn Sharing:** Share hymn lyrics and stanzas directly with family, friends, or congregation members.

### 📜 2. Holy Bible (King James Version)
- Complete Old Testament and New Testament books (Genesis through Revelation).
- Clean chapter and verse navigation with intuitive reading layout.
- Parallel devotional context references.

### 🕊️ 3. Daily Devotional & Spiritual Growth
- Daily curated devotional readings with Scripture readings, Memory Verses, Reflections, Prayers, and Action Points.
- Personal devotional notes journal.
- Daily reading streaks to keep you motivated and faithful in your spiritual journey.

### 🔍 4. Ultra-Fast Offline Search (FTS5)
- Search instantly by **hymn number**, **title**, **first line**, or **words in lyrics**.
- Search the Holy Bible across all books and chapters.
- 100% local SQLite Full-Text Search (FTS5) — zero lag, zero data consumption.

### 📌 5. Personal Study & Library Tools
- **Bookmarks & Favorites:** Save important hymns and Bible verses with one tap.
- **Personal Notes:** Jot down personal thoughts, sermon notes, and study reflections.
- **Reading History:** Pick up right where you left off.
- **Customizable Typography:** Dynamic font sizing for comfortable reading across all screen sizes.
- **Light & Dark Theme:** Beautifully styled for bright morning church services or dim nighttime devotions.
- **Keep-Awake Mode:** Prevent screen timeouts during church singing or extended Bible reading.

### ⏰ 6. Gentle Worship & Devotional Reminders
- Set personalized reminders for daily devotionals, Bible study, and prayer times.
- Includes custom spiritual reminder tones (*Act, Pray, Read, Wake*).

### 🔒 7. Privacy-First & 100% Offline
- **No Sign-Up or Login Required:** Open the app and begin worshiping immediately.
- **Zero Internet Required:** All hymns, Bible books, and devotionals are bundled locally.
- **Zero Data Collection:** No trackers, no ads, no profiling. All personal notes stay on your device.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Specifications
- **Framework:** React Native Expo (SDK 57)
- **Engine:** React 19 / Hermes / New Architecture
- **Local Storage:** SQLite (`content.db`, `kjv.sqlite`, `devotionals.db`, `user.db`)
- **Navigation:** Expo Router file-based navigation
