Privacy Policy

Your privacy is sacred to us. J.H.S Bible & Hymnal ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and providing a peaceful, spiritual reading experience unburdened by digital surveillance, profiling, or tracking.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal data or non-personal data whatsoever. Specifically, the Application:

  • Does NOT collect Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification.
  • Does NOT require user authentication: No user accounts, passwords, social logins, or cloud synchronization profiles.
  • Does NOT track device identifiers: No IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID (AAID), MAC address, or serial numbers.
  • Does NOT collect location data: No GPS coordinates, IP-derived geo-location, or network cell data.
  • Does NOT collect financial information: The app is free of subscriptions, paywalls, and in-app financial transactions.
  • Does NOT collect behavioral or usage analytics: We do not monitor how often you open the app, which hymns you sing, or which scriptures you read.

2. Local Data Storage on Your Device

To enhance your personal study and worship, the Application provides features allowing you to save bookmarks, mark favorite hymns, take devotional study notes, and track your daily reading streaks.

Important: All your notes, bookmarks, favorites, and reading preferences are saved exclusively on your physical device in a private, sandboxed SQLite database (user.db). This data is never uploaded, synced to an external server, or made accessible to us or any third party.

3. Data Retention and Deletion (User Control)

Because all data remains strictly on your local device under your own custody:

  • Full User Control: You can edit or delete individual bookmarks, notes, or favorites directly from within the app's Library and Settings screens at any time.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Clearing the Application's storage via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > J.H.S Bible & Hymnal > Storage > Clear Storage) or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local databases and saved records from your device immediately.

4. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertisements

The Application contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, NO analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics), and NO social media tracking plugins. It communicates with zero external APIs during its execution.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

The Application is designed for general audiences, families, and churches. It contains purely religious, educational, and spiritual text. Because the Application collects no personal data whatsoever from any individual, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future app enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Because the app operates offline, we encourage you to review this policy periodically.

  • Parameterized Local SQL: All queries to local SQLite tables (user.db, content.db) are strictly parameterized, protecting against local script injection.
